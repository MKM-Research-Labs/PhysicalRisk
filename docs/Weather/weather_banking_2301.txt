23.1 The Trader's Day
================================================================

SECTION PURPOSE
---------------
Chapter 22 showed where PRS fits in bank infrastructure. This chapter shows how
it runs day-to-day. This section uses the PoC's 7-tab trader workstation as the
operational template for what a PRS market-maker actually does.

THE WORKSTATION
----------------
The PoC provides a complete trader's workstation with seven functional tabs,
each mapping to a specific phase of the trading day:

  Tab 1: TRADE BLOTTER
  The trader's primary view. Shows all open PRS positions with:
  - SwapID, gauge, trigger level, notional, direction (payer/receiver)
  - Trade spread vs current fair spread
  - Mark-to-market (MTM)
  - Gauge FS01 (sensitivity to 1bp hazard rate move)
  - Basis DV01 (sensitivity to basis spread change)
  - Daily P&L (new trade + market move)
  - Running P&L (inception-to-date cumulative)
  - Counterparty name and trade date

  Summary bar: total notional, aggregate daily P&L, aggregate running P&L,
  broken down by new trade vs market move contribution.

  Tab 2: RISK GRID
  P&L exposure by gauge x trigger level, ordered geographically (west to east
  along the catchment). Heat map showing concentration risk:
  - Which gauges carry the most FS01 exposure
  - Where the portfolio is long vs short flood risk
  - Where a single storm event creates correlated losses

  Tab 3: TRADE MAP
  Geographic overlay of positions on the catchment map. Visual identification
  of spatial concentration, proximity to gauges, and overlap with property
  portfolios. Connects trading positions to the physical geography.

  Tab 4: YIELD CURVE & HAZARD TERM STRUCTURE
  Curve management interface:
  - View and adjust yield curve used for discounting
  - View and adjust hazard term structure per gauge
  - Commit curve changes (saved) or reset to base case
  - Curve history showing adjustment timeline

  Intraday: trader adjusts hazard rates based on weather information, EA flood
  alerts, or market intelligence. This is analogous to a CDS trader adjusting
  credit spread assumptions as new information arrives.

  Tab 5: P&L TIME SERIES & CURVE HISTORY
  Historical view:
  - Daily P&L per swap over time
  - Cumulative P&L evolution
  - Curve adjustment history (when were hazard rates bumped, by how much)
  - Attribution: how much P&L came from new trades vs market moves

  Tab 6: END-OF-DAY (EOD)
  EOD submission workflow:
  - Trigger EOD snapshot at close of business
  - All positions revalued at EOD hazard rates
  - Portfolio P&L summary computed and signed off
  - EOD report PDF generated (position detail, P&L chart, market state)
  - Historical EOD submissions viewable for audit trail

  Tab 7: STRESS TESTING
  Storm scenario analysis:
  - Select gauge and storm scenario from catalogue
  - View classifier training status per gauge
  - Run stress: storm -> hydrograph -> P(flood) -> stressed P&L
  - Portfolio-level stress across all positions
  - Stress waterfall chart and summary statistics

A TYPICAL TRADING DAY
-----------------------

  07:30 MORNING REVIEW
  - Open blotter (Tab 1): review overnight MTM changes
  - Check which gauges moved: did any EA alerts fire overnight?
  - Review daily P&L attribution: what drove yesterday's P&L?
  - Check risk grid (Tab 2): any concentration limits approaching?

  08:00-09:00 MARKET ASSESSMENT
  - Review weather forecasts and EA flood outlook
  - Assess hazard term structure (Tab 4): do current hazard rates reflect
    latest information?
  - If significant weather change: adjust hazard rates intraday
  - Revalue portfolio against updated rates

  09:00-16:00 INTRADAY TRADING
  - Receive pricing enquiries from counterparties
  - Price new PRS: select gauge, trigger level, tenor, notional
  - Pricing engine produces: fair spread, basis adjustments, indicative price
  - Negotiate spread with counterparty
  - Commit trade: CDM record created, confirmation PDF generated
  - Monitor blotter as positions update with new fair spreads

  16:00-17:00 CLOSE-OUT AND EOD
  - Close-out any positions being unwound (reverse trade at agreed spread)
  - Review full day's P&L
  - Submit EOD (Tab 6): snapshot, sign-off, report generation
  - Run daily stress (Tab 7): check portfolio against storm catalogue

  AD HOC: FLOOD EVENT
  - EA issues severe flood warning at a gauge
  - Gauge monitor service alerts the desk
  - Evaluate GaugeSet: does this breach trigger any open PRS positions?
  - If triggered: initiate settlement process
  - Simultaneously: assess portfolio impact via property-level cascade

This daily workflow mirrors a CDS trading desk. The trader thinks in spreads,
manages risk via sensitivities (FS01 instead of CS01), attributes P&L to market
moves, and submits EOD snapshots. The only operationally new element is the
gauge monitoring service and the weather-driven hazard rate assessment.
