23.4 Flood Event Response
================================================================

SECTION PURPOSE
---------------
Detail what happens operationally when a gauge breaches its trigger level --
the real-time event response that distinguishes PRS from CDS. Show the dual
notification cascade: gauge-level (PRS settlement) and property-level
(mortgage book impact).

THE TRIGGER EVENT
------------------
A PRS trigger event occurs when an EA gauge reading exceeds the severe flood
warning threshold specified in the GaugeSet of an open PRS trade.

Unlike CDS credit events, PRS triggers are:
- Binary: the gauge either exceeded the threshold or it did not
- Objective: measured by government-calibrated instruments
- Verifiable: published in real time by the Environment Agency (15-min intervals)
- Immediate: no Determination Committee adjudication required
- Auditable: full historical record maintained by EA

This simplicity is a structural advantage over CDS, where credit event
determination for restructuring events requires subjective Determination
Committee adjudication (15 members: 10 dealers + 5 buy-side, per Big Bang
Protocol). PRS settlement is closer to CDS bankruptcy triggers, which are
also binary and factual.

GAUGE-LEVEL RESPONSE (PRS Settlement)
---------------------------------------
When a gauge breaches its severe threshold:

  1. DETECTION
     - Gauge monitor service receives 15-minute update from EA API
     - Current reading compared against trigger levels in all open GaugeSets
     - Alert raised if reading >= severe_threshold for any referenced gauge

  2. GAUGESET EVALUATION
     For each open PRS referencing the breached gauge:
     - Retrieve GaugeSet from PhysicalSwap CDM record
     - Apply trigger logic:
       * "Any": single gauge breach triggers payout (most common, PoC default)
       * "All": every gauge in basket must breach
       * "Weighted": weighted sum of breached gauges exceeds threshold
       * "Majority": more than half of basket must breach
     - If trigger conditions met: position enters settlement

  3. PAYOUT CALCULATION
     - Payout amount = PayoutSevereFlood from GaugeSet for the breached gauge
     - For tiered payouts:
       * Alert level payout (lowest tier)
       * Warning level payout (middle tier)
       * Severe level payout (highest tier, full notional)
     - Settlement amount determined by which tier was breached

  4. SETTLEMENT
     - T+2 cash settlement (standard CDS convention)
     - Protection seller pays payout amount to protection buyer
     - Trade status updated to "Triggered" then "Settled"
     - CDM record updated with event timestamp, gauge reading, settlement amount
     - Confirmation PDF generated documenting the trigger event

  5. POST-SETTLEMENT
     - No further premiums due from protection buyer (contract terminated)
     - No further protection from seller
     - Final P&L computed and recorded in trade marks
     - Position removed from active blotter

PROPERTY-LEVEL RESPONSE (Mortgage Book Impact)
------------------------------------------------
Simultaneously with PRS settlement, the flood event cascades through the
mortgage book:

  1. SPATIAL PROPAGATION
     - Spatial model maps gauge breach to property-level flood depths
     - KDTree identifies all properties within the affected zone
     - IDW interpolation estimates flood depth at each property location
     - Properties within ~1000m of gauge share strongly correlated impacts
     - Distance decay function reduces estimated depth with distance

  2. DAMAGE ESTIMATION
     - Depth-damage functions (JRC, MCM, or calibrated library) convert
       estimated flood depth to damage ratio (0-1) per property
     - Damage ratio x property value = estimated structural loss
     - Contents loss estimated separately (typically 30-50% of structural)
     - Business interruption duration estimated from depth and property type

  3. MORTGAGE IMPACT
     - For each affected property with a linked mortgage:
       * Property value adjusted: value_post_flood = value - damage
       * LTV recalculated: new_LTV = outstanding_balance / value_post_flood
       * If new_LTV > 100%: property in negative equity
       * Affordability ratio adjusted for increased insurance costs
       * Borrower PD updated via the capital markets approach (Chapter 11)
       * LGD updated via Frye-Jacobs (Chapter 17):
         cLGD = Phi((Phi^-1(EL/PD) - sqrt(rho) x Phi^-1(cDR)) / sqrt(1-rho))

  4. PORTFOLIO IMPACT
     - Aggregate across all affected mortgages:
       * Total collateral impairment
       * Number of properties in negative equity
       * Portfolio-level PD and LGD shifts
       * ECL adjustment = Sum(cPD x cLGD x EAD) across affected loans
       * Capital impact: change in RWA from updated PD/LGD
     - If PRS protection is in place:
       * PRS payout offsets collateral impairment
       * Adjusted LGD = max(0, base_LGD - PRS_recovery / EAD)
       * Capital relief quantified

  5. CLAIMS REPORTING
     - The PoC generates automated claim reports per property:
       * Property details (from Property CDM)
       * Estimated flood depth and damage ratio
       * Structural and contents loss estimates
       * Mortgage impact (LTV change, negative equity status)
       * Recovery timeline estimate

THE DUAL CASCADE IN PRACTICE
------------------------------
When the Reading gauge breaches 7.25m (severe flood warning):

  Gauge-level:
  - All open PRS referencing Reading with severe trigger are evaluated
  - Trigger type "Any": single gauge breach -> payout activated
  - Protection buyers receive PayoutSevereFlood amount (T+2)
  - Positions settle and close

  Property-level:
  - Spatial model estimates flood depths across ~200 properties near Reading
  - Properties within 500m: estimated 0.5-1.5m depth
  - Properties within 1km: estimated 0.1-0.5m depth
  - Depth-damage: 0.5m depth -> ~15-25% damage ratio on residential
  - A GBP 350K property with 0.5m flooding loses ~GBP 60-90K value
  - LTV on a GBP 250K mortgage: 71% -> 92% (or into negative equity at 1m+)
  - Frye-Jacobs: if portfolio conditional PD rises from 1.5% to 6%,
    cLGD rises from 25% to ~38%, ECL nearly doubles

  The PRS payout arrives at T+2 to the protection buyer (the mortgage lender),
  providing immediate recovery that reduces the Frye-Jacobs LGD adjustment
  and stabilises the portfolio capital position.

COMPARISON TO CDS CREDIT EVENT
---------------------------------
  Aspect              | CDS                        | PRS
  --------------------|----------------------------|---------------------------
  Trigger detection   | DC adjudication (weeks)    | Gauge reading (immediate)
  Objectivity         | Subjective for restructuring| Binary, verifiable
  Settlement timing   | Auction + T+3              | T+2
  Recovery rate       | Market-determined auction   | Pre-defined by tier
  Notification chain  | DC -> participants         | EA -> gauge monitor -> desk
  Secondary impact    | Sector contagion           | Property-level cascade
