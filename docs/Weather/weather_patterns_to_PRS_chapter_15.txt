Chapter 15: Theoretical Use Case (pp. 409-416)
================================================================

CORE THESIS
-----------
A fully worked Thames Valley PRS pricing example demonstrating that CDS pricing mechanics
from Chapter 12 can be operationally applied to flood risk using EA gauges as reference
entities. Two-step approach: 1-year baseline from annual exceedance probabilities, then
multi-year extension using Gumbel heavy-tail accumulation.

A 5-year Reading PRS on GBP 1M notional costs GBP 82,914 p.a. (total GBP 414,570).
Gumbel approach produces roughly 2x the premium of simple exponential scaling.

KEY ARGUMENTS
-------------
1. Five Thames gauges provide comprehensive coverage
   - Oxford (40yr return, 2.5% AEP), Reading (30yr, 3.3%), Windsor (20yr, 5.0%),
     London Bridge (100yr, 1.0%), Tilbury (200yr, 0.5%)
   - Triggers at EA "severe flood warning" thresholds

2. Premium structure follows CDS conventions with flood-specific components
   - Pure Risk Cost = AEP x Payoff (e.g., 3.3% x GBP 1M = GBP 33K)
   - Risk Margin: 15% of pure cost
   - Operating Costs: 13% of risk-adjusted cost
   - Capital Cost: GBP 18K p.a. (15% of payoff at 12% cost of capital)

3. Multi-year pricing uses CDS survival mechanics
   - Premium leg: Sum(Annual_Premium x DF(i) x Survival(i))
   - Protection leg: Sum(Notional x Survival(i) x AEP x DF(i))
   - 3-year Reading: PV Premium = GBP 166,816; PV Protection = GBP 89,711
   - Implied spread = 53.8%

4. Gumbel accumulation is financially material
   - Reading 5yr: Simple 15.45% vs Gumbel 31.88% (106% difference)
   - Windsor 5yr: Simple 22.6% vs Gumbel 44.8% (98% difference)

5. Geographic basis risk creates spread adjustment framework
   - Base index 55bp + distance +3bp + elevation -1bp + terrain +2bp
     + model uncertainty +2bp = 61bp portfolio-specific

KEY MODELS AND FRAMEWORKS
--------------------------
- 1-Year Premium = AEP x Notional x (1+risk_margin) x (1+op_cost) + capital_cost
- PV Premium Leg(i) = Annual_Premium x DF(i) x Survival(i)
- PV Protection Leg(i) = Notional x Survival(i) x AEP x DF(i)
- Implied Spread = PV_Protection / PV_Premium
- Illiquidity premium: 2% (3yr), 3% (5yr)
- Survival probability from Gumbel formula (Ch 14)
- Discount rate: 3% (default-free)
- Basis: index_spread + distance + elevation + terrain + model_uncertainty

FORWARD REFERENCES
------------------
- Extensions to other river systems "follow naturally from this framework"
- Standardisation across rivers creates "foundation for indices and portfolio products"
- References Basel III capital requirements for PRS protection sellers (Ch 16)

CDS INFRASTRUCTURE ANALOGY
---------------------------
STRONGEST evidence for the analogy. Pricing mechanics directly mapped: premium leg = CDS
premium leg; protection leg = CDS protection leg; survival = CDS survival curve; trigger =
credit event; implied spread = CDS spread. Settlement is fixed/digital rather than recovery-
based, actually simplifying mechanics relative to CDS.
