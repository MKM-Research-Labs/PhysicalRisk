Chapter 11: Mortgage Risk Assessment (pp. 360-371)
================================================================

CORE THESIS
-----------
Mortgage valuation requires three complementary frameworks -- DCF with PD/LGD integration,
OAS methodology, and stochastic house price models -- but a fourth approach drawn from CDS
pricing methodology offers distinct advantages for physical risk. This "capital markets
approach" centres on affordability ratios as the primary default driver, using piecewise
functions where credit spreads increase linearly at low ratios (<15%), steepen moderately
(15-30%), and grow exponentially above 30%.

Physical hazards impact mortgages through three channels: increased insurance costs
(affordability), temporary income disruption (recovery), and property value decline
(recovery value). The CDS-style approach handles these naturally, producing outputs
(credit spreads, hazard rates, survival probabilities, expected loss) directly compatible
with bank credit risk systems.

KEY ARGUMENTS
-------------
1. Two sources of default: "inability to pay" (income shocks) and "strategic default"
   (leaving keys when property becomes uninsurable)
2. DCF with PD/LGD: ECF(t) = Scheduled(t) x (1-PD(t)) - Balance(t) x PD(t) x LGD(t)
3. OAS approach: treats default as embedded option; Monte Carlo simulation
4. Stochastic model: house prices follow GBM, interest rates mean-revert, correlated
5. Capital markets (CDS) approach: affordability -> credit spread -> hazard rate
   h = 1 - exp(-spread/2); survival probabilities recursive
6. Physical risk advantage: CDS approach naturally incorporates flood impacts through
   insurance parameter, income disruption, and recovery haircuts

KEY MODELS AND FRAMEWORKS
--------------------------
- DCF with PD/LGD integration
- OAS methodology
- Stochastic house price (GBM) + interest rate (Vasicek-type)
- Capital markets approach: affordability-to-spread piecewise function
- Hazard rate: h = 1 - exp(-spread/2)

FORWARD REFERENCES
------------------
- CDS approach described as "a valuable bridge between traditional mortgage valuation
  and forward-looking physical risk assessment"
- Outputs (credit spreads, hazard rates, survival probabilities) directly usable for PRS

CDS INFRASTRUCTURE ANALOGY
---------------------------
Strongest structural support. The capital markets approach explicitly uses CDS pricing
mechanics for mortgage valuation. Outputs are identical in structure to CDS outputs.
Bank credit risk systems can process both CDS and PRS-adjusted mortgage valuations
without architectural changes. The mathematical translation is not analogical but structural.

INTEGRATION COST SIGNALS
-------------------------
Standard/reusable: DCF framework, PD/LGD methodology, Monte Carlo, hazard rate /
survival probability calculations, OAS methodology -- all exist in bank systems
Novel/new-build: Affordability ratio piecewise function calibrated to flood risk,
insurance parameter dynamic adjustment, gauge-trigger-to-mortgage transmission model,
physical risk haircut estimation
