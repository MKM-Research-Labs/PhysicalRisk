Chapter 13: Physical Risk Swaps (pp. 384-399)
================================================================

CORE THESIS
-----------
PRS is presented as a straightforward extension of credit risk infrastructure, not an
exotic commodity derivative. The pricing mathematics are identical (hazard rates become
flood probabilities, survival curves become no-flood probabilities, premium/protection
legs unchanged). The legal and operational infrastructure (ISDA, FINOS CDM, DRR) is
adopted wholesale. Banks' existing XVA desks can process PRS without modification.

Three product tiers mirror CDS evolution: Index PRS (single-gauge, 55bp), Portfolio PRS
(multi-gauge weighted, 63bp with basis adjustments), PRO Tranches (CDO-style with
equity/mezzanine/senior at 180bp/85bp/35bp).

KEY ARGUMENTS
-------------
1. Pricing mathematics translate directly from CDS
   - Premium Leg = Sum(S x Delta_t_i x DF_i x PS_i)
   - Protection Leg = (1-R) x Sum((PS_{i-1} - PS_i) x DF_i)
   - PRS: identical with h(t) = flood hazard rate, PS(t) = e^(-int h(s)ds)
   - Big Bang conventions (upfront + fixed coupon) apply directly

2. Return periods translate to hazard curves
   - P(X>=1) = 1 - e^(-t/tau); lambda = -ln(1-p); lambda ~ p for small p

3. PRS triggers are more objective than CDS
   - Gauge either exceeded threshold or did not -- binary, verifiable
   - No Determination Committee subjectivity for restructuring events

4. Three product tiers mirror CDS evolution
   - Index PRS: 55bp, single gauge, 1-day execution, high basis risk
   - Portfolio PRS: 63bp (+8bp basis), multi-gauge weighted, zero basis risk
   - Basis decomposition: model uncertainty (+/-2bp), distance (+1 to +6bp),
     terrain (-1 to +3bp), elevation (-2 to 0bp), composition (-1 to +2bp)
   - PRO Tranches: equity (0-0.2m, 180bp), mezzanine (0.2-1.0m, 85bp),
     senior (1.0m+, 35bp). Blended cost 64.5bp

5. Complete CDS-to-PRS service line mapping
   - Every IHS Markit/S&P CDS service has a PRS equivalent: pricing data,
     reference entity data, indices, liquidity metrics, event services,
     trade processing, portfolio valuations, regulatory support

6. Implementation leverages existing infrastructure entirely
   - Legal: ISDA Master Agreement, modular physical risk definitions
   - Data: FINOS CDM for trade lifecycle; gauge triggers encoded in CDM
   - Regulatory: FINOS DRR compliant from first transaction
   - Monitoring: EA gauge network (no new infrastructure)
   - Clearing: early central clearing via existing CCPs

KEY MODELS AND FRAMEWORKS
--------------------------
- PRS pricing: identical CDS formula (premium leg = protection leg)
- Hazard rate: h(t) = conditional flood probability; PS(t) = e^(-int h(s)ds)

- Three-tier product: Index, Portfolio, PRO Tranches
- Basis decomposition framework (5 components)
- CDS-to-PRS service line mapping table

CDS INFRASTRUCTURE ANALOGY
---------------------------
This chapter completes the analogy and converts it into architectural specification.
The mapping is not metaphorical -- it is one-to-one across pricing mathematics, legal
framework, operational infrastructure, and commercial service lines. PRS "maps identically
to CDS for market instrument standards, market infrastructure, and bank risk architecture."

PRS differs favourably in trigger objectivity (binary gauge readings vs subjective DC
adjudication). The genuinely new capability is the physical risk science layer: gauge-
specific hazard curves, spatial correlation, climate projection integration, basis risk.
But this science layer plugs into existing bank infrastructure at the same interface
points where credit risk analytics currently connect.
