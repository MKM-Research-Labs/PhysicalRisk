22.5 Risk and P&L Systems
================================================================

SECTION PURPOSE
---------------
Show that daily mark-to-market, Greeks, P&L decomposition, EOD workflow, and
close-out settlement for PRS are structurally identical to CDS. Detail the
DeltaEngine and PnLEngine implementations as evidence.

DAILY MARK-TO-MARKET
----------------------
The PoC's DeltaEngine revalues every open PRS position against current hazard rates:

  MTM = (fair_spread - trade_spread) / 10,000 x risky_annuity x notional x direction

Where:
- fair_spread: current model-implied spread from updated hazard curve
- trade_spread: the spread at which the trade was executed
- risky_annuity: Sum(dt x S(t) x DF(t)) over quarterly periods
- direction: +1 for protection buyer (payer), -1 for protection seller

This is identical to CDS mark-to-market:
  CDS MTM = (market_spread - trade_spread) / 10,000 x risky_annuity x notional

The only difference is the source of "market spread": in CDS it comes from
observed market quotes; in PRS it comes from the hazard curve builder. As PRS
markets develop and traded spreads become available, PRS MTM will converge to
market-observable pricing -- exactly as CDS evolved.

GREEKS (SENSITIVITIES)
-----------------------
The PoC computes two sensitivities per position:

  Gauge FS01 (Flood Spread 01):
  - Sensitivity to a 1bp parallel shift in the gauge's hazard rate
  - Computed by bumping: (spread(lambda+1bp) - spread(lambda)) / 10,000
    x annuity x notional
  - Analogous to CS01 (Credit Spread 01) in CDS

  Basis DV01:
  - Sensitivity to a 1bp change in the property-to-gauge basis spread
  - Under the PoC's constant-basis assumption, basis_dv01 ~ gauge_dv01
  - Analogous to CDS-bond basis sensitivity

The risk grid aggregates FS01 across gauge x trigger level dimensions, providing
a portfolio heat map of flood-spread exposure ordered geographically (west to east
along the catchment). This is structurally identical to a CDS risk grid showing
CS01 by reference entity x maturity.

P&L DECOMPOSITION
------------------
The PoC's PnLEngine decomposes daily P&L into:

  new_trade_pnl:  full MTM if traded today, else 0
  market_pnl:     today's MTM minus yesterday's MTM (hazard curve moves)
  daily_pnl:      new_trade + market
  running_pnl:    cumulative from inception
  realized_pnl:   final P&L on closed trades

This is the standard P&L attribution used on any derivatives desk:
- New trade P&L: day-one P&L from trading at off-market levels
- Market move P&L: P&L from changes in hazard rates (flood risk repricing)
- Running P&L: inception-to-date cumulative

A bank's existing P&L infrastructure can process PRS P&L in the same format
it uses for CDS, with "hazard curve move" replacing "credit spread move" as
the market risk factor.

CLOSE-OUT MECHANICS
--------------------
The PoC implements close-out as a reverse trade:

  1. Find original trade by SwapID
  2. Compute full revaluation MTM at close-out spread:
     settlement = (closeout_spread - trade_spread) / 10,000 x annuity x notional x direction
  3. Update trade status to "Closed"
  4. Save: close_date, close_spread_bps, final_pnl, settlement_amount
  5. Compute T+2 settlement date (skip weekends)
  6. Generate close-out confirmation PDF
  7. New CDM record created with CloseOutOf referencing original SwapID

This mirrors CDS unwind mechanics: bilateral agreement on a close-out spread,
computation of settlement amount based on the difference from original trade
spread, T+2 cash settlement.

EOD WORKFLOW
-------------
The PoC generates daily EOD snapshots:

  EOD Snapshot:
  +-- eod_id: "EOD-YYYYMMDD"
  +-- date: snapshot date
  +-- market_state_snapshot: hazard rates, yield curve, basis adjustments
  +-- portfolio_summary: total notional, daily P&L by source, running P&L
  +-- positions: per-trade detail (fair spread, deltas, MTM, P&L)

  EOD submission triggers:
  - Revaluation of all open trades at EOD hazard rates
  - Portfolio P&L summary computation
  - JSON snapshot saved to eod_dir/
  - PDF report generated with position detail and P&L time series chart

This EOD process is identical to what a CDS desk runs: end-of-day revaluation,
P&L sign-off, position reporting, snapshot for regulatory and audit purposes.

FRYE-JACOBS LGD INTEGRATION
-----------------------------
Chapter 17 established that physical events create compounding PD-LGD dynamics
via the Frye-Jacobs model:
  cLGD = Phi((Phi^-1(EL/PD) - sqrt(rho) x Phi^-1(cDR)) / sqrt(1-rho))

PRS directly addresses this by providing a payout at the point of physical event
-- before the PD-LGD correlation fully materialises. When computing capital
relief, the PRS payout reduces conditional LGD:
  adjusted_LGD = max(0, base_LGD - PRS_recovery_amount / EAD)

This feeds into the capital relief formula from Chapter 16:
  Capital relief = delta(credit risk capital) - [counterparty + CVA + market risk capital on PRS]

The risk system must integrate PRS payouts into the Frye-Jacobs framework for
stress scenarios, showing that PRS reduces the compounding PD-LGD effect that
drives 30-50% of incremental scenario losses.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR INTEGRATION
---------------------------------
Every component of PRS risk management -- MTM, Greeks, P&L decomposition,
close-out, EOD -- uses the same mathematical operations and data flows as CDS.
A bank does not need new risk infrastructure for PRS. It needs to:
1. Add "flood hazard rate" as a new risk factor type (alongside credit spread)
2. Configure P&L attribution to recognise hazard curve moves
3. Add PRS positions to existing EOD revaluation batch
4. Extend the stress testing framework with storm scenarios (section 22.7)
5. Integrate PRS payouts into Frye-Jacobs LGD calculations for capital relief
