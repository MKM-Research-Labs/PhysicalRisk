Chapter 17: Climate Stress Testing (pp. 437-462)
================================================================

CORE THESIS
-----------
The climate stress testing landscape is fragmented across dozens of institutions, but two
specific contributions are operationally useful: the UNEP FI credit risk methodology
(representative borrower approach, climate credit quality index) and the Frye-Jacobs LGD
model. Frye-Jacobs is the centrepiece: it derives scenario-conditional LGD from through-
the-cycle parameters already maintained for Basel IRB compliance.

The core insight: physical climate events generate conditions where PD and LGD move
together most severely. Flooding simultaneously impairs creditworthiness AND destroys
collateral, while insurance gaps transfer losses to lenders. LGD adjustments typically
contribute 30-50% of incremental scenario losses beyond PD-only models.

KEY ARGUMENTS
-------------
1. Institutional landscape is specialised but creates implementation burden
   - FSB coordinates; NGFS develops scenarios; UNEP FI provides credit methodology
   - Banks must build reusable components, not parallel processes per framework

2. UNEP FI bridges climate scenarios to credit risk
   - Representative borrower approach; climate credit quality index
   - Scenario-adjusted PD = Phi(Phi^-1(TTC_PD) + beta*Phi^-1(rho*Climate_Index))

3. Traditional LGD treatment is doubly inadequate for physical climate risk
   - 2008 crisis was demand collapse, not physical destruction
   - Katrina: recovery rates below 30% in worst areas
   - Thailand 2011: commercial recovery 20-30 cents on the pound

4. Frye-Jacobs provides systematic PD-LGD link
   - cLGD = Phi((Phi^-1(EL/PD) - sqrt(rho)*Phi^-1(cDR)) / sqrt(1-rho))
   - Uses only Basel IRB parameters (PD, ELGD, rho)
   - Worked example: coastal mortgage portfolio, baseline PD=1.5%, ELGD=25%,
     rho=15%. Flood scenario cPD=8% -> cLGD=43% (72% increase from 25% baseline)
     ECL jumps from 2.0% to 3.4% of exposure

5. Physical events amplify PD-LGD correlation through unique channels
   - Simultaneous collateral destruction, insurance gaps, secondary market evaporation
   - Persistent: flood-affected properties show impaired recovery for 3-5 years

6. Validation requires alternative approaches since backtesting is impossible
   - Theoretical soundness, parameter reasonableness, historical benchmarking

KEY MODELS AND FRAMEWORKS
--------------------------
- Frye-Jacobs: cLGD = Phi((Phi^-1(EL/PD) - sqrt(rho)*Phi^-1(cDR)) / sqrt(1-rho))
- LGD Risk Index: k = (Phi^-1(EL) - sqrt(rho)*Phi^-1(PD)) / sqrt(1-rho)
- UNEP FI Climate Credit Quality Index
- Basel IRB asset correlation formula
- ECL = Conditional_PD x Conditional_LGD x EAD

CDS INFRASTRUCTURE ANALOGY
---------------------------
Provides loss-modelling justification for PRS. Frye-Jacobs shows physical events create
compounding PD-LGD dynamics unmanageable through traditional credit tools. PRS directly
addresses this by providing payout at point of physical event -- before PD-LGD correlation
fully materialises. Analogous to how CDS provides recovery certainty at credit event,
preventing workout uncertainty.

Complication: CDS pays based on actual credit event of reference entity; PRS pays on
gauge trigger that may not perfectly correlate with borrower's actual loss (basis risk).
