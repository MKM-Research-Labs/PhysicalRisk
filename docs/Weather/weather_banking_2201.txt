22.1 The Integration Thesis
================================================================

SECTION PURPOSE
---------------
This section establishes the central claim: PRS is not a new asset class requiring
new infrastructure -- it is a new product type on existing CDS infrastructure. The
proof-of-concept platform demonstrates this by building a functioning trading desk
on CDS pricing mechanics, CDM trade records, and ISDA conventions.

THE ARGUMENT
------------
The preceding chapters have built a complete pipeline from atmospheric science
(Chapters 2-3) through hydrology and hydraulics (Chapters 5-6), flood risk
assessment (Chapters 7-8), insurance and mortgage risk (Chapters 9-11), to the CDS
framework (Chapter 12) and its PRS extension (Chapter 13). They have established
the regulatory architecture (Chapter 16), the stress testing methodology (Chapter
17), the governance framework (Chapter 19), the CDM data model (Chapter 20), and
the FDC3 interoperability layer (Chapter 21).

Chapter 21 concluded with an explicit promise: this chapter would show "Phase 6 of
the MKM Bayesian Monte Carlo framework -- the specific mechanisms through which
precipitation forecasts transform into banking risk metrics, leveraging these
interoperability standards to achieve operational deployment within existing banking
infrastructure."

This is that chapter. The integration thesis rests on three observations:

1. THE PRICING ENGINE IS IDENTICAL
   The PoC implements PRS pricing using the same quarterly CDS mechanics described
   in Chapter 12. The fair spread calculation is:
     fair_spread = protection_leg_pv / risky_annuity x 10,000 bps
   where:
     risky_annuity = Sum(dt x S(t) x DF(t)) over quarterly periods
     protection_leg = (1-R) x Sum((S(t-1) - S(t)) x DF(t))
     S(t) = exp(-lambda x t), lambda = -ln(1 - annual_hazard_rate)
   This is not analogous to CDS pricing -- it IS CDS pricing with a different
   hazard rate source. A bank's existing QuantLib CDS engine can compute PRS
   spreads by replacing credit hazard rates with flood exceedance probabilities.

2. THE TRADE RECORD IS CDM-COMPLIANT
   Every PRS trade committed in the PoC creates a PhysicalSwap CDM record with
   Header (SwapID, CounterParty, TradeStatus), LegData (Fixed, GBP, ACT/360),
   ScheduleData (T+2 settlement, semi-annual rolls, London calendar), GaugeSet
   (gauge basket with trigger levels), Triggers, Payouts, and Pricing sections.
   This record structure mirrors OTC derivatives booking and can flow through
   existing trade capture systems with the GaugeSet as the only new element.

3. THE RISK INFRASTRUCTURE IS REUSABLE
   Mark-to-market, Greeks (FS01 per gauge, basis DV01), P&L decomposition (new
   trade vs market move vs running), EOD snapshots, and close-out settlement all
   use the same mechanics as a CDS desk. The DeltaEngine computes sensitivities
   by bumping hazard rates -- identical to CDS spread sensitivity.

WHAT THE POC DEMONSTRATES
--------------------------
The proof-of-concept is not a toy. It implements:
- Full trade lifecycle: price -> commit -> daily MTM -> close-out -> settlement
- 7-tab trader workstation: blotter, risk grid, trade map, curves, P&L, EOD, stress
- CDM records for PRS, properties, mortgages, gauges, counterparties, storm events
- 16 analytical models with documentation, parameter inventories, test suites
- Stress testing with 260 historical storm scenarios and per-gauge GBM classifiers
- Model governance: MRC workflow, audit trail, BCBS 239 compliance, RACI matrix
- 6,047 automated tests with coverage reporting

Its purpose is to let a trader "feel" PRS trading before the bank commits to
integration -- and to prove that the integration cost is incremental, not
transformational.

THE DELTA ANALYSIS
------------------
For a bank with existing CDS infrastructure, the integration can be framed as a
delta -- what must be added to what already exists:

REUSE AS-IS:
- ISDA Master Agreement (modular extension with physical risk supplement)
- QuantLib CDS pricing engine (swap hazard rate source)
- Trade booking systems (add PhysicalSwap as new product type)
- Counterparty credit risk and CVA calculations
- Mark-to-market and margin/collateral frameworks
- P&L decomposition and attribution
- EOD and regulatory reporting infrastructure
- Central clearing connectivity (ICE/LCH)
- Compression services (TriOptima)

ADD NEW:
- Gauge reference entity database (analogous to Markit RED codes)
- GEV hazard curve construction from EA gauge data
- Basis waterfall engine (5 components: model uncertainty, distance, elevation,
  terrain, composition)
- GaugeSet concept in CDM (basket of gauges with trigger levels)
- EA gauge telemetry feed (public, free, 15-minute updates)
- Physical risk ISDA definitions supplement
- Flood classifier per gauge (GBM, for stress testing)
- Storm scenario catalogue (for stress testing)

The "add new" list is bounded and specific. None of it requires replacing existing
systems -- it extends them.
