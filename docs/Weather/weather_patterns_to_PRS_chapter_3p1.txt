Chapter 3.1: Weather - Initialization (pp. 98-109)
================================================================

CORE THESIS
-----------
Every scenario in the Weather Monte Carlo framework begins as a sample from a
physics-informed prior distribution p(X_0). This chapter constructs that prior
through three components: (a) the atmospheric state vector definition, (b) physical
constraints via conservation and balance laws that prevent "impossible weather,"
and (c) a hierarchical Bayesian structure for extreme events that pools information
across global, regional, and local scales to estimate tail probabilities even when
local observations are sparse.

KEY MATHEMATICAL MODELS
-----------------------
1. State Vector and Prior
   - X_0 = [T_0, p_0, u_0, v_0, w_0, q_0, ...] (temperature, pressure, winds,
     vertical velocity, humidity)
   - X_0 ~ N(mu_0, Sigma_0) -- multivariate normal prior
   - mu_0: best estimate from NWP analyses + climatology
   - Sigma_0: encodes uncertainty, spatial correlation, cross-variable relationships
   - Dimension: millions (all grid points x all variables)

2. Conservation Laws (Four Constraints)
   - Mass: continuity equation (no air appearing from nowhere)
   - Energy: first law of thermodynamics for atmospheric parcels
   - Momentum: Newton's second law with Coriolis, pressure gradient, gravity, friction
   - Moisture: dq/dt = E - P + div(moisture flux), linked to Clausius-Clapeyron

3. Balance Relationships
   - Geostrophic: f x v ~ -(1/rho) grad p (wind-pressure consistency)
   - Hydrostatic: dp/dz = -rho g (vertical pressure structure)
   - Thermal wind: vertical wind shear linked to horizontal temperature gradient

4. Spatial Correlation Structure
   - Corr(X_i, X_j) = exp(-d_ij / L), with L = 50-200 km for synoptic features
   - Ensures nearby grid points are strongly correlated

5. Hierarchical Bayesian Extremes
   - Three-level hierarchy: global -> regional -> local
   - GEV for block maxima (location mu, scale sigma, shape xi)
   - GPD for threshold exceedances
   - Shape parameter cascades: xi_local ~ N(xi_regional, tau^2_local)
   - Solves the fundamental problem that 100-year events rarely appear in 50-year
     local datasets -- information is pooled across spatial scales

6. Extreme Event Detection
   - Historical analogue matching
   - Compound extreme detection
   - Precursor recognition (12-48 hour pattern library preceding floods)
   - Trajectory divergence analysis

7. Validation
   - Compare empirical quantiles Q_emp(p) against prior quantiles Q_prior(p)

FORWARD REFERENCES
------------------

- Hierarchical GEV/GPD structure feeds tail estimation for IDF curves in 3.5

CDS INFRASTRUCTURE ANALOGY
---------------------------
The GEV/GPD hierarchy is analogous to credit rating transition matrices -- both are
calibrated from historical data, both involve pooling across entities, both have
tail-sensitivity problems. But the atmospheric prior is vastly higher-dimensional
and must be recalibrated continuously against physical observations, not just
default data. This is significant operational overhead beyond current CDS
infrastructure.
