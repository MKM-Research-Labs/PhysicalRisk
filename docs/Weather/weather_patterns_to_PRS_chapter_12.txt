Chapter 12: Credit Default Swaps (pp. 372-383)
================================================================

CORE THESIS
-----------
The CDS market's thirty-year evolution -- from JPMorgan's 1994 capital relief innovation
through the $62.2T peak, the 2008 crisis, and comprehensive post-crisis reform -- is
presented not as history but as a proven template PRS must follow. Every piece of CDS
infrastructure is catalogued and declared available for PRS reuse. The critical lesson:
PRS can skip the crisis phase entirely by adopting mature infrastructure from day one.

KEY ARGUMENTS
-------------
1. Origin as capital relief tool: JPMorgan's 1994 CDS transferred Exxon credit risk to
   EBRD, freeing $384M in Basel I capital. BISTRO (1997) scaled to $9.7B portfolio
   coverage. Capital relief is the same driver for PRS adoption by mortgage lenders.

2. Growth outpaced infrastructure: $632B to $62.2T (2001-2007). Confirmation backlogs
   hit 21 business days. PRS must launch with infrastructure, not add it later.

3. Markit as pricing/reference infrastructure: RED codes (6-digit entity, 9-digit pair),
   ~2,200 priced entities, CDX/iTraxx index administration with semi-annual rolls.
   PRS equivalent: gauge reference database, PRS pricing data, physical risk indices.

4. ISDA legal standardisation: 2003 Definitions, Big Bang Protocol (March 2009):
   auction settlement hardwiring, Determination Committees (10 dealers + 5 buy-side),
   fixed coupons (100bp IG / 500bp HY), quarterly IMM dates (20th Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec),
   60-day credit event lookback, 90-day succession lookback. 2014 Definitions:
   Senior/Sub split, Asset Package Delivery, Universal Successor.

5. Central clearing: ICE Clear Credit (March 2009), LCH CDSClear. 75-80% now cleared.
   ICE processed $1.1T in single day (Sept 2024).

6. Compression: TriOptima eliminated $524T+ by 2015. Lehman auction: $400B gross to
   $5.2B net (recovery 8.625%). Market: $62.2T to ~$9.3T through compression.

KEY MODELS AND FRAMEWORKS
--------------------------
- CDS pricing: premium leg vs protection leg, par spread
- Big Bang Protocol: 100bp/500bp coupons, IMM dates, auction settlement, DCs
- Markit RED codes, CDX/iTraxx indices
- ICE Clear Credit / LCH CDSClear
- TriOptima triReduce compression
- ISDA 2003 and 2014 Credit Derivatives Definitions
- DTCC Trade Information Warehouse

CDS INFRASTRUCTURE ANALOGY
---------------------------
This chapter IS the analogy. Written specifically to catalogue CDS infrastructure and
declare it available for PRS. PRS has a structural advantage: can launch with mature
infrastructure. Gauge-based triggers (binary, verifiable) are simpler than CDS credit
event determination (subjective Determination Committee adjudication for restructuring).
