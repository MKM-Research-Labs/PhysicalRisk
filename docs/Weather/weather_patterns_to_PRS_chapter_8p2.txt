Chapter 8.2: From Flood Depth to Loss (pp. 294-302)
================================================================

CORE THESIS
-----------
Damage functions are the translation layer where hydrological science meets balance-sheet
reality. Without them, scientifically rigorous flood maps cannot price risk, allocate
capital, or evaluate mitigation. Damage functions are not physical laws but statistical
summaries of past building responses, supplemented by engineering judgment, and must be
governed as models under SR 11-7.

Depth is the primary intensity variable because it is physically meaningful, reliably
modellable, and critically -- the variable for which post-event calibration data exists.

KEY ARGUMENTS
-------------
1. Depth-damage curves are the workhorse of flood loss estimation
   - Input: inundation depth (metres); Output: damage ratio (0-1) or absolute loss
   - Vary by building type, construction, contents, use

2. Three construction approaches dominate
   - Empirical: post-event survey/claims data (HOWAS21/FLEMO, Germany)
   - Synthetic/expert-based: component-level engineering (JRC, UK MCM, HEC-FIA)
   - Hybrid/ML: combine empirical + expert using regression, Random Forests, Bayesian

3. Key function libraries
   - JRC Global (Huizinga et al.): worldwide, good for screening, weak locally
   - HOWAS21/FLEMO (Germany): most detailed empirical resource
   - HEC-FIA/USACE (US): standardised US occupancy-class curves
   - UK Multi-Coloured Manual: includes coastal/fluvial variants and resilience effects
   - Oasis LMF and OS-Climate/FINOS physrisk: open-source

4. Damage functions are model-risk problems, not parameter choices
   - Must document intended use, lineage, applicability domain, limitations
   - Validation: reasonableness tests, sensitivity analysis, transfer assumptions

KEY MODELS AND FRAMEWORKS
--------------------------
- Depth-damage curve: depth -> damage ratio x exposure = loss
- JRC Global, HOWAS21/FLEMO, HEC-FIA, UK MCM
- Oasis LMF, OS-Climate/FINOS physrisk
- SR 11-7 governance for damage functions

CDS INFRASTRUCTURE ANALOGY
---------------------------
Damage functions are the PRS equivalent of recovery rate models in CDS. In CDS, recovery
rate converts default event into loss amount; in PRS, damage function converts flood depth
into loss amount. Both are empirically calibrated and governed as models. CDS recovery
rates are estimated with few variables; damage functions depend on depth, building type,
construction, contents, resilience -- a higher-dimensional input space.
