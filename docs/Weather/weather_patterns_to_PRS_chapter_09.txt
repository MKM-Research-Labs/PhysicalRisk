Chapter 9: Insurance Risk Assessment (pp. 312-338)
================================================================

CORE THESIS
-----------
Insurance calculation is the critical junction where hazard science converges with
financial risk transfer. Insurance's one-year horizon creates both a powerful market
signal about the true cost of hazards and a dangerous temporal mismatch with mortgages
extending risk over decades. When insurers withdraw or raise premiums beyond affordability,
mortgage exposures are left unprotected -- precisely the gap PRS fills.

The chapter directly states: "parametric insurance is the foundation for designing a
Physical Risk Swap."

KEY ARGUMENTS
-------------
1. The fundamental premium equation structures all insurance pricing
   - Premium = Pure Premium + Expense Loading + Risk Loading + Profit Margin
   - Pure premium: credibility theory PP = Z*Individual + (1-Z)*Class, Z=n/(n+K)
   - Risk loading = CoC x (TVaR_99.5 - Expected Loss)

2. Territorial rating evolved from zones to continuous ML-driven risk surfaces
   - 5m resolution, coordinate-based, different premiums in same postcode

3. Multivariate class rating uses GLMs: log(Premium) = beta_0 + sum(beta_i*X_i)
   - Extensions: GAMs, mixed-effects, elastic net, ensemble ML

4. Parametric pricing is the bridge to PRS
   - Objective triggers (depth, distance, satellite indices) replace subjective claims
   - Reduces disputes, increases transparency

5. CDMs (FINOS + OASIS) enable standardised property definitions and transparent pricing

6. Insurance retreat from high-risk markets creates systemic vulnerability
   - Collateral devaluation 3.6-10%, mortgage delinquencies up 15-30%

---------------------------

risk premium + liquidity). Credibility theory mirrors entity-specific vs sector CDS
blending. Parametric triggers eliminate claims adjustment, making PRS settlements as
clean as CDS credit event determinations.
