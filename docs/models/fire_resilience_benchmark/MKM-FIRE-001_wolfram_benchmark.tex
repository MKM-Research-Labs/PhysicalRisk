\mkmexecsummary
% =========================================================================

This document benchmarks the Building Fire-Resilience Credit Model (MKM-FIRE-001),
which prices a commercial asset's fire resilience as a containment rate, an annual
loss frequency and a full-conflagration spread for the Commercial PRS Pricer. The
model estimates all three by Monte Carlo. This benchmark computes them
\emph{exactly}: the per-fire jitter makes the point-of-no-return step a random
variable whose law is a product of lognormal tail probabilities, and given that step
the containment chain is a matrix product, so each credit is a finite sum.

\paragraph{The model is right, and its tables are honest.} Model C reproduces its
documented formulae to \bmProfile{} over \bmAssets{} distinct assets. The code, run at
the document's own draw count and seed, reproduces \bmReproduced{} of the
\bmPrinted{} figures printed in its Tables 1--12, and every printed figure lies within
\bmWorstZ{} standard errors of the exact credit (Table~\ref{tab:bm-cells}).
\bmWolframConfirms

\paragraph{Findings} (Table~\ref{tab:bm-claims}):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{The production spread is a noisy draw.} Production runs \bmProductionN{}
  draws, and the spread is the fraction of draws that reach the point of no return.
  At the reach cliff the exact spread is \bmCliff\,bp; at \bmProductionN{} draws its
  standard error is \bmCliffSE\,bp, yet the pricer quotes it to 0.1\,bp
  (Table~\ref{tab:bm-production}). The exact credit takes about a tenth of a second
  per asset, so it can replace the simulation for the spread.
\item \textbf{The cliff is \bmCliff\,bp, not 203.} The printed 203 is a sampled estimate
  of $10^4(1-e^{-\lambda})$; it is within sampling error, but it is not the value.
\item \textbf{FIRE-L3, measured.} With no effective suppression the race is lost in
  \bmRaceLost\% of fires, but the chain has already absorbed in all but
  \bmRaceRecorded\%; the conflagration leg sees only those. The document's own
  limitation is confirmed and now quantified.
\item \textbf{The tall non-combustible residual does not exist.} \S8 and Table 12
  expect single-digit bps ``in very tall non-combustible assets''. The exact residual
  is \bmTall\,bp at 20 storeys, the same as at 12: above the reach, suppression is
  unreachable at any height, and nothing else in the race depends on storeys.
\item \textbf{Some of the printed variation is noise.} Tables 4, 5 and 9 print
  containment rates from \bmNoiseLow\% to \bmNoiseHigh\% across conditions, types and
  occupancies. Those inputs change only how often a fire starts, not how it ends: the
  exact containment rate is \bmNoiseExact\% in every row. At 20{,}000 draws a cell
  rests on a few hundred fires, and the differences are sampling error.
\item \textbf{Two statements in the document differ from the code.} Suppression
  becomes active at $\lceil\tau_{\mathrm{det}}+\tau_{\mathrm{supp}}\rceil$, not
  $\lceil\tau_{\mathrm{supp}}\rceil$ (\S3.3); without detection in that sum the
  detection level would do nothing at all. And every transition matrix carries an
  $S_6\to S_7$ escalation row that the march never reads, because it stops at $S_6$
  (\S3.5).
\end{enumerate}
The chain absorbs in \bmAbsorb{} steps on average and intensity climbs \bmGrowth{} a
step at the baseline, as \S7.1 says; the non-combustible residual at 12 storeys is at
most \bmResidual\,bp, sub-bp as Table 12 says; and Table 13 is reproduced from the
documented formulae.

\subsection{What is benchmarked}
In \texttt{src/models/fire/}: Model C (\texttt{response\_effectiveness.py}), the
intensity race of Model B (\texttt{intensity\_track.py}), the containment chain and
the end-to-end \texttt{simulate\_asset\_fire} of Model D (\texttt{containment.py}),
and the draw-level ignition of Model A (\texttt{initiation.py}), each cell built as
the document's sensitivity generator built it.

\subsection{What is not}
Model A's modifier tables are taken as the configuration of record: the benchmark
uses the code's $\lambda_i$ and checks what is done with it. The initiation class,
which the document notes is cosmetic, does not enter the credit. Every parameter is
an engineering-judgement seed (FIRE-L1); this benchmark shows the model computes
what it documents, not that the seeds are right. It addresses validation question
Q8, not independent validation (Q9).

\mkmframework
% =========================================================================

\paragraph{The race.} Before suppression bites, intensity is $\min(t\,g',C')$, with
$g'=g\,e^{\sigma_g Z_1}$ and $C'=I_{\max}e^{\sigma_c Z_3}$, and suppression bites at
$\tau_{\mathrm{det}}+\tau'$, $\tau'=\tau_{\mathrm{supp}}e^{\sigma_\tau Z_2}$, the $Z_i$
independent standard normals. The point of no return latches at the first $t$ with
$t\,g'>I_{\mathrm{crit}}$, provided $C'>I_{\mathrm{crit}}$ and $t<\tau_{\mathrm{det}}+\tau'$
(at $t=1$ for a combustible frame suppression cannot reach). So
\begin{equation}
P(K=t)=P\!\left(\tfrac{I_{\mathrm{crit}}}{t}<g'\le\tfrac{I_{\mathrm{crit}}}{t-1}\right)
P\!\left(\tau'>t-\tau_{\mathrm{det}}\right)P\!\left(C'>I_{\mathrm{crit}}\right),
\end{equation}
each factor a lognormal tail.

\paragraph{The chain.} With $M=wM_{AA}+(1-w)M_{\mathrm{weak}}$ before the latch and
$M_{\mathrm{PNR}}$ from it, and the terminal states made absorbing,
\begin{equation}
\pi=\sum_{t=1}^{T}P(K=t)\;e_0M^{t-1}M_{\mathrm{PNR}}^{\,T-t+1}+P(K>T)\;e_0M^{T},
\end{equation}
with $T=200$ the step budget; a point of no return is recorded only if the chain is
still transient after $t-1$ steps. The credit is the containment rate $\pi_{S_5}$,
the loss frequency $q(\pi_{S_6}+\pi_{S_7})$ and the spread $10^4\,q\,P(\mathrm{PNR\ recorded})$,
with $q=1-e^{-\lambda h}$ the probability a draw ignites.

% =========================================================================
\section{Benchmark Method}
% =========================================================================

Model C and the two sums above are evaluated with \texttt{mpmath} at 50 digits, from
the document rather than the code; lognormal tails are taken from \texttt{erfc}, so
a $10^{-140}$ probability keeps its digits. Wolfram evaluates the same sums
independently --- the race law from \texttt{Erfc} survival functions, the chain with
\texttt{FoldList} and \texttt{MatrixPower} --- for each of the \bmAssets{} distinct
assets behind the \bmCells{} printed cells. The model runs each cell at
\bmNSim{} draws with seed \bmSeed, as the generator did, and each of its figures is
judged in standard errors of the exact value; the containment rate's on the expected
number of fires.
