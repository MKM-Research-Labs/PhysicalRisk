\noindent\textbf{Interpretation:}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item At sub-alert levels (4.0--5.0\,m), $P(\text{flood}) \approx 0$
          regardless of hour, consistent with the GBM surface.
    \item At the severe threshold (6.58\,m), $P(\text{flood})$ ranges from
          0.42 at hour~0 (high uncertainty, storm just started) to 0.97 at
          hour~167 (near certainty, storm almost over).
    \item Above the severe level (7.0+\,m), $P(\text{flood})$ saturates
          toward 1.0, reflecting overwhelming evidence of flood breach.
    \item The hour dimension provides meaningful discrimination primarily in
          the transition zone (5.5--6.6\,m), where early-hour uncertainty is
          largest.
\end{itemize}
