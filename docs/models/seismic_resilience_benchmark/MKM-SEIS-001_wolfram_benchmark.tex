\mkmexecsummary
% =========================================================================

This document benchmarks the Building Seismic-Resilience Credit Model
(MKM-SEIS-001), which prices a commercial asset's seismic resilience as a
no-collapse rate, an annual loss frequency and a full-collapse (DS3) spread for the
Commercial PRS Pricer. The model estimates all three by Monte Carlo. This benchmark
computes them \emph{exactly}: with the source-to-site distance fixed, an event's
damage state depends on its magnitude, the GMPE residual and one fragility uniform,
and the last two integrate out in closed form, leaving one integral over the
truncated Gutenberg--Richter law per damage state.

\paragraph{The model is right, and its tables are honest.} Model C, the GMPE median
and the occurrence rate match the reference to \bmCheckErr. The code, run at the
document's own draw count and seed, reproduces \bmReproduced{} of the \bmPrinted{}
figures printed in its Tables 3--10, and every printed figure lies within \bmWorstZ{}
standard errors of the exact credit (Table~\ref{tab:bm-cells}). All six acceptance
gates of Table~2 hold on the exact credit, without the 0.7\,bp tolerance the
generator allows for noise. \bmWolframConfirms

\paragraph{Findings} (Table~\ref{tab:bm-claims}):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{The tables are too noisy to read cell by cell.} At \bmNSim{} draws one
  collapse is 0.5\,bp, and a \bmStableExact\,bp spread carries a \bmStableSE\,bp standard
  error (\bmStableRel\%) --- so \S9.6's ``stability is adequate at $n_{\mathrm{sim}}=20{,}000$''
  does not hold. \bmReversals{} adjacent pairs in the tables run against the exact order,
  among them Table~9's Very-high row, where B prints above NR: all are sampling
  noise (Table~\ref{tab:bm-noise}).
\item \textbf{The production spread is noisy too.} Production runs \bmProductionN{} draws;
  the largest exact spread, \bmProdExact\,bp, then carries a \bmProdSE\,bp standard error.
  The exact credit takes under a second per asset.
\item \textbf{Table 18's ``Mean L'' are medians.} The loss of DS0--DS2 is a lognormal
  with that \emph{median}, clipped (not truncated) at 1, so its mean is higher: DS2
  averages \bmMeanTwo, not 0.35, before the post-earthquake-fire cascade adds more.
\item \textbf{The GMPE is not specified.} \S3.3 and Appendix~B name a
  Boore--Atkinson-style proxy; the coded form, with magnitude-dependent geometric
  spreading, appears only in the code.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What is benchmarked}
In \texttt{src/models/seismic/}: occurrence and the fault geometry (Model A), the GMPE
(Model B), the BRI modifiers (Model C) and the damage, loss and cascade of Model D,
end to end through \texttt{simulate\_asset\_seismic}, each cell built as the document's
sensitivity generator built it: a 2\,km east--west fault with the asset due south of
its midpoint.

\subsection{What is not}
Table~11 prices the Halong portfolio from catchment data not available to this run.
Assets with a long fault and an off-centre position, where $R_{JB}$ varies with the
rupture, are outside the generator's cases and this benchmark's. The functionality
index and PML outputs do not enter the credit. Every parameter is an
engineering-judgement seed (SEIS-L1). This addresses validation question Q8, not Q9.

\mkmframework
% =========================================================================

With $M$ truncated Gutenberg--Richter on $[m_{\min},m_{\max}]$ with slope $b$, the coded
GMPE median $\mu(M,R,V_{S30})$, the soft-soil factor $F_a$, residual sigma $\sigma$, and
Model-C-adjusted medians $\theta_i$ with dispersion $\beta$, one uniform against the
nested fragility curves gives
\begin{equation}
P(\mathrm{DS}\ge i\mid M)=\Phi\!\left(\frac{\ln\mu(M)+\ln F_a-\ln\theta_i}{\sqrt{\sigma^2+\beta^2}}\right),
\qquad
p_i=\int_{m_{\min}}^{m_{\max}}P(\mathrm{DS}\ge i\mid m)\,f_M(m)\,\mathrm{d}m .
\end{equation}
With $q=1-e^{-\lambda h}$ the probability a draw has an event, the spread is $10^4q\,p_3$
and the no-collapse rate $1-p_3$. The loss of DS0--DS2 is $\min(\tilde m e^{sZ},1)$,
whose mean is
$\tilde m e^{s^2/2}\Phi(-\ln\tilde m/s-s)+\Phi(\ln\tilde m/s)$; the cascade replaces it
with the larger of it and the fire loss, whose mean is
$\int_0^1\bigl(1-F_S(x)F_F(x)\bigr)\mathrm{d}x$; the loss frequency is
$q\sum_k P(\mathrm{DS}=k)\,E[L\mid k]$.

% =========================================================================
\section{Benchmark Method}
% =========================================================================

The integrals are evaluated with \texttt{mpmath} at 50 digits, from the document and
the configuration rather than the code; the GMPE takes the form the code documents,
the document giving none. Wolfram evaluates the damage-state integrals independently
with \texttt{NIntegrate}, for each of the \bmAssets{} distinct assets behind the
\bmCells{} printed cells. The model runs each cell at \bmNSim{} draws with seed \bmSeed,
as the generator did, and each figure is judged in standard errors of the exact
value: the no-collapse rate's on the expected number of events, the loss frequency's
from the exact second moment, and the median PGA's from the density at the median.
